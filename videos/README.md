# Moving backgrounds (2026-10-24)
Put looping videos here (MP4 with H.264 or HEVC works best; WebM also works). Sub-folders become albums.
- Keep each file under 100 MB (GitHub's limit). A 20–60 second loop at 4K HEVC is usually 30–90 MB.
- The TV plays them muted and loops them seamlessly when the clip's first and last frames match.
- YouTube links can't be played directly; download the clip (from a source you're allowed to use) and upload it here.
